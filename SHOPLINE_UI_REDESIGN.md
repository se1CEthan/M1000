# SelTech Online - Shopline-Inspired UI/UX Redesign

## Design System Overview

### Color Palette (Inspired by Shopline)
- **Primary**: #0066FF (Vibrant Blue)
- **Primary Dark**: #0052CC
- **Primary Light**: #E6F0FF
- **Secondary**: #00C896 (Success Green)
- **Accent**: #FF6B35 (Warm Orange)
- **Background**: #FFFFFF
- **Surface**: #F8FAFC
- **Text Primary**: #1A202C
- **Text Secondary**: #64748B
- **Border**: #E2E8F0

### Typography
- **Headings**: Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif
- **Body**: Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif
- **Monospace**: 'JetBrains Mono', monospace

### Design Principles
1. **Clean & Minimal**: Lots of white space, clear hierarchy
2. **Modern Gradients**: Subtle gradient backgrounds
3. **Smooth Animations**: Micro-interactions and transitions
4. **Card-Based Layout**: Elevated cards with subtle shadows
5. **Bold CTAs**: Large, prominent call-to-action buttons
6. **Professional Icons**: Consistent icon system
7. **Responsive Grid**: Mobile-first approach
