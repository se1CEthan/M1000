## What Sellers Need to Fill In to Receive 90% of Sales

### 🎯 **Quick Setup (2 Minutes)**

To receive **90% of every sale automatically**, sellers only need to fill in **ONE thing**:

## 📝 **Required Information**

### **Option 1: Cryptocurrency Wallet (RECOMMENDED - Fastest)**
- **Wallet Address**: Your crypto wallet address
- **Currency**: Choose from USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH
- **Network**: TRC20 (for USDT) or ERC20 (for USDC)

**Example:**
```
Wallet Address: TQn9Y2khEsLMWD2iRuHjEzSvX8zStF2LeA
Currency: USDT
Network: TRC20
```

### **Option 2: PayPal Account**
- **PayPal Email**: Your PayPal account email

**Example:**
```
PayPal Email: seller@example.com
```

### **Option 3: Bank Account**
- **Account Number**: Your bank account number
- **Bank Details**: Bank name and routing info

### **Option 4: Wise Transfer**
- **Wise Email**: Your Wise account email

---

## 🚀 **How to Set Up (Step by Step)**

### **Step 1: Go to Seller Dashboard**
1. Login to your account
2. Click "Seller Dashboard" in the menu
3. Go to "Payout Configuration" section

### **Step 2: Add Payout Method**
1. Click "Add Payout Method"
2. Choose your preferred method (Crypto recommended)
3. Fill in your wallet address or account details
4. Click "Add Payout Method"

### **Step 3: That's It! 🎉**
- Your payout method is now configured
- You'll automatically receive 90% of every sale
- Payments arrive in 10-30 minutes (crypto) or 1-3 days (other methods)
